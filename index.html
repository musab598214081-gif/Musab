Ringing this phone

This phone won't ring yet. Right now it only rings while this page is open in front of you. To ring with the page closed, install the free app ntfy (say "notify") and follow the steps below.

A web page cannot ring a phone that is closed — only an installed app can. ntfy (pronounced "notify") is a small free app that does exactly that job for anything that needs it, and this page uses it as its doorbell. No account, no cost, open source.

Search ntfy on the App Store or Play Store and install it, then come back and tap the button below.

On Android this opens ntfy with the topic filled in. On iPhone the topic is copied instead — open ntfy, tap +, and paste it.

Ring like a call on iPhone (Bark)

ntfy can't keep an iPhone ringing, or ring it on silent. The free app Bark can: calls ring for 30 seconds, even on silent and Do Not Disturb. ntfy keeps working alongside it.

1 · Install Bark from the App Store and open it.
2 · Allow notifications, and allow Critical Alerts when asked (or later: Settings → Apps → Bark → Notifications → Critical Alerts).
3 · In Bark, copy the address shown on its first screen — it looks like https://api.day.app/AbCd… — and paste it here.

Leave this alone unless the check above fails on every network you use. Some office and school wifi blocks ntfy.sh outright — if yours does, run your own ntfy and put its address here. It travels in the invite links, so everyone moves together.

Without one, everybody on your wifi shares a single small allowance and calls start being refused after a busy evening. A free account at ntfy.sh gets its own. Sign up, go to Account → Access tokens → Create, and paste the tk_… token here. It travels in the invite links so the whole family uses it.

Ringtone

How this phone sounds when somebody calls or buzzes you. Tap one to hear it. This is your own setting — it doesn't change what anyone else hears.

Relay server

Call audio goes phone to phone. On some mobile networks the two phones cannot reach each other directly and need a relay in the middle. Free from openrelay.metered.ca (20 GB a month): sign up, create a TURN credential, and paste the credentials link it shows - the one ending in ?apiKey=… - below. No username or password needed with the link.

Additional relay (optional)

A second relay, used together with the one above - the call takes whichever connects best, and if one is down or blocked the other carries it. Made for Cloudflare (1,000 GB a month free): deploy the small Worker file that came with this app, then paste its address here.
